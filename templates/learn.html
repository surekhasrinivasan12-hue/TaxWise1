📚 Learn About Income Tax

Understand income tax in a simple and easy way before using the calculator.

What You Will Learn

Income tax can look complicated at first. This guide explains the basic concepts step-by-step so that you can understand how tax calculations work.

  • What is a tax regime?

💰

1. What is Income Tax?

Income tax is a tax charged on taxable income according to the applicable tax laws.

Individuals may have income from sources such as salary, business, profession, house property, investments and other sources.

Simple idea: Income earned → Applicable tax rules → Tax calculated
🧾

2. What is Taxable Income?

Taxable income is the income considered for calculating income tax after applying the applicable deductions, exemptions and rules.

Simple idea:

Gross Income − Applicable deductions/exemptions = Taxable Income

The exact calculation depends on the applicable tax rules and the type of income.

📊

3. What are Tax Slabs?

A tax slab is an income range to which a particular tax rate applies.

Tax is calculated progressively. This means that different portions of income can be taxed at different rates.

💡 A higher slab rate does not mean that the entire income is automatically taxed at that highest rate.

4. What is a Tax Regime?

A tax regime refers to the set of tax rates, deductions, exemptions and rules used for calculating income tax.

New Tax Regime

The new regime has its own slab structure and provides certain benefits according to the applicable tax rules.

Old Tax Regime

The old regime has a different slab structure and allows eligible deductions and exemptions subject to the applicable rules.

🧮

5. How is Income Tax Calculated?

A simplified tax calculation can be understood through the following steps:

  1. Identify the taxable income.
  2. Select the applicable tax regime.
  3. Divide income into the applicable tax slabs.
  4. Calculate tax for each applicable slab.
  5. Apply an eligible rebate, if applicable.
  6. Calculate applicable cess.
  7. Obtain the estimated final tax.
Basic calculation idea:

Slab Tax − Eligible Rebate = Tax After Rebate

Tax After Rebate + Applicable Cess = Estimated Final Tax
🎁

6. What is a Rebate?

A rebate can reduce the tax payable when the taxpayer satisfies the conditions prescribed under the applicable tax rules.

For the new regime implemented in this educational application, an eligible taxpayer within the applicable income limit may receive a Section 87A rebate.

💡 Rebate reduces the tax payable. It is different from a deduction.
➕

7. What is Health and Education Cess?

Health and Education Cess is an additional amount calculated on applicable income tax according to the prescribed rate.

In this educational calculator:

Cess = Tax After Rebate × 4%
🔍

8. Why Does the Calculator Show a Breakdown?

TaxWise does not simply display a final number. It shows how the estimated tax was calculated.

The result page displays:

  • Taxable income
  • Tax before rebate
  • Eligible rebate
  • Tax after rebate
  • Health and Education Cess
  • Estimated final tax

This helps the user understand where the final tax amount comes from.
