FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TaxWise an official government website?

No. TaxWise is an independent educational and tax-estimation website. It is not the official Income Tax Department portal.

Which assessment year does TaxWise support?

This version is designed around Assessment Year 2026–27.

Why does TaxWise ask for my age?

Age is relevant to the old tax regime because the basic exemption limit differs for individuals below 60, senior citizens and super senior citizens.

Can I claim 80C and 80D in the new regime?

The deductions supported in this version are considered only when the Old Tax Regime is selected. Actual tax eligibility can depend on your personal circumstances and the applicable law.

Is the result my exact tax liability?

No. The result is an estimate based on the inputs and rules supported by this version. It does not cover every income type, exemption, deduction, special tax rate, surcharge, marginal relief or filing situation.

⚠️ Important

TaxWise is an educational tax-estimation tool. The calculation should not be treated as a substitute for professional tax advice or official income-tax filing.
